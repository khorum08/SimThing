## BUILD
- Admit exactly one generic primitive, full-domain `EXP`, through the landed 5.10 `ExactPrimitiveAdmissionDoor`. Pin the algorithm-as-spec and add only the required closed-opcode, CPU-twin, interpreter, and SSA-JIT lowering surfaces.
- Keep semantics reproducible rather than pretending correct rounding: the pinned sequence is the bit law, but characterize it independently as an exponential so deterministic nonsense cannot pass merely by agreeing with itself.
- Produce the local exhaustive-certification artifacts and trust-chain freshness wiring; standing CI watches those artifacts but never re-executes the 2^32 qualification.
- Exercise the two named consumers as authored EML data: sign-stable CostBand steering and exponential STEAD falloff. Add the EXP-only stabilized gadget forms; leave LN-derived library work to 5.12.
## FENCES
- One primitive, one admission door, one interpreter/JIT vocabulary, one certified bit semantics. `CLOSED_OPCODES` widens only for `EXP`; no semantic opcode, bespoke kernel, second cost model, or second proof system.
- Domain admission remains exactly the 5.10 split: range certificate OR explicitly guarded semantics. Guarding changes authored meaning; it never proves the unguarded source was valid.
- Exhaustive qualification is local and phase-boundary; CI is freshness-only. Cost is judged by the existing exact-primitive resource-effect key against the real gadget baseline, unweakened.
- `EXP` semantics are append-only once admitted. No `LN`, `POW`, `eml()`, log-domain accumulation, StemThing, movement, or contention work rides this landing.
## EXIT-PROOF
- Exactly one `EXP` primitive is admitted over the full finite domain; CPU, interpreted GPU, and JIT are bit-identical to the pinned sequence, and a planted fusion/reassociation mutant reds the digest.
- Every certified toolchain has a pinned exhaustive 2^32 digest/replay artifact whose trust-chain freshness is mechanically watched without CI re-execution; independent numerical characterization records the approximation envelope without claiming correct rounding.
- The existing cost key proves no regression plus at least one strict compiled-resource improvement versus the pinned gadget encoding. Stabilized Logistic/Softmax and the CostBand/STEAD consumers execute through existing EML surfaces with no bespoke path.
