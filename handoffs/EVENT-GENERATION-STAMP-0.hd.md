## BUILD
- **The stamp is the seam primitive, not bookkeeping.** It is the prerequisite for independently-executing subtrees, so it must carry on BOTH surfaces: emission/threshold events AND **reduce-up products**. A parent integrating a child's product without knowing its generation is summing state from mixed times; integrating an unstamped product is a hard error, not a tolerated default.
- **Async is ADMITTED AS ORDINARY.** A parent at generation N+3 integrating a child's gen-N product is the NORMAL case. It must complete with no error, no warning, no degraded path, and above all **no wait**. Requiring the parent to wait is synchronous execution under another name and destroys the capability this rung exists to create.
- **Determinism is preserved by RECORDING, not by WAITING.** Determinism holds relative to a **recorded integration schedule** — which stamped products were integrated at which parent generation. That is authored/recorded data, not ambient timing. Replay the schedule, reproduce exactly. This is the same move the track makes everywhere: what varies becomes DATA.
- **Staleness becomes VISIBLE rather than silent.** The stamp is what makes a stale integration observable and attributable; the recorded schedule is what makes the resulting heatmap a reproducible training corpus rather than an artifact of which host happened to be slow.
- **Stamped ring egress with admission-time backpressure.** Forced observer lag honors declared backpressure without perturbing the sim.
- **BINDS 6.0b — preservation only.** `AttachOverlay` already routes through `deliver_routed_overlay` with a required `origin`. The stamp rides that existing arrival; it does not get its own path, and dispatch may not regress to direct attachment.
## FENCES
- Every dispatch-minted overlay carries `UntilDissolved` with an **authored** dissolve condition (Definable Horizon Law). A runtime overlay with no dissolve condition accretes in the hottest loop in the system rather than in a TSV — make it unconstructible or hard-error it at admission. No permanence variant; `AtSessionEnd` is a definable horizon, never "never".
- No second stamp carrier, clock, global barrier, sequence authority, or scheduler. The stamp rides existing emission and reduce-up. The recorded integration schedule is THE single replay recorder -- 6.2 will extend it with a row kind, never a second log -- and its rows are PER-PRODUCT, preserving the full generation set of what was integrated, never per-bucket-latest: values sum under later coalescing but stamps do not, and a schedule that records only the latest stamp loses which generations merged and cannot replay bit-exactly.
