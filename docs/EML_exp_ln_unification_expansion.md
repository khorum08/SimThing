## 0. Executive verdict

The live EML substrate has also advanced since the first draft: EXP and LN are now admitted exact primitives on their certified domains, and 5.14 has specified arm-independent EML arithmetic semantics while deleting per-consumer exactness policing. This removes the earlier risk that the architectural proposal depended on an unresolved LN implementation and gives a future tiled executor the correct status: **one more faithful lowering of the existing language, never a semantic fork.**

The strongest result of the exploration is **not** that SimThing needs an intrinsic contention subsystem, nor that every action should become a new `ActionBand` object. The stronger and more economical conclusion is:

> **The existing CostBand hinge, intrinsic RF claim/clear/disburse behavior, field-derived action potential, and StemThing derivation/residency budget appear to be partial projections of one recurring constrained-action law.**

For reference in this workshop that law is called **ActionBand**.

ActionBand is not proposed as a fifth StemThing leg, a domain service, a destination planner, a new graph, or necessarily even a new runtime type. It is a **composition law** over existing mechanisms:

```text
perception / state / deficit
        ↓
STEAD / PALMA / RF potential
        ↓
local gradients / candidate progress
        ↓
EML valuation and arbitration
        ↓
resource-backed claim
        ↓
intrinsic parent clear / disburse
        ↓
provisional grant
        ↓
CostBand
        ↓
determinate progress depth N
        ↓
ordinary state transition
        OR
existing boundary structural mutation
        ↓
next-generation state / pressure
```

This revision adopts the central Fable/Sol critiques:

1. **Do not canonize a `ContentionCostBand` mechanism.** Contention is the oversubscribed posture of ordinary constrained clearing.
2. **Do not conflate unresolved claim with CostBand remainder.** `U = claim - allocation` and `R = V - N*C` are different quantities with different semantics.
3. **Do not treat attrition as a peer clearing algorithm by default.** Clearing determines what distributes now; attrition is better tested as costly persistence across generations.
4. **Do not introduce same-generation convergence.** `observe → claim → clear → commit → state` runs once in the admitted generation ordering; consequences recurse through later generations.
5. **Keep conserved resources, derived signals, and committed claims mechanically distinct.**
6. **Keep structural mutation outside the numerical executor.** Numeric evaluation may authorize a structural consequence; the existing barrier owns the mutation.
7. **Do not let the tile architecture become semantic authority.** After 5.14, a tile is only another faithful physical lowering of one EML language.
8. **Measure the known locality debt before building a general tensor machine.** Shared-ingress/workgroup-local field execution is the first performance falsifier.

This revision nevertheless pushes beyond the aggregate review's narrower recommendation that only 8.2 attrition needs correction.

### 0.1 Owner hypothesis worth DA review

There is substantial architectural evidence that **full intrinsic unification is justified at the law level**:

- CostBand is already the singular sink/action boundary.
- The StemThing derivation surface is already explicitly `Derivation-as-CostBand`.
- Residency is already a conserved contested lane whose claims reduce up and grants disburse down.
- 8.2 already proposes claim → resolution → disbursement as generic executed RF.
- 7.1 already requires movement-consuming actions to use CostBand.
- STEAD/PALMA already provide the field/gradient substrate from which action pressure and direction arise.
- The SimThing principle already says apparently separate domains collapse into one recursive GPU automaton.

The likely unification is therefore not:

```text
CostBand + ActionBand + DerivationBudget
```

as three sibling mechanisms.

It is:

```text
ACTION POTENTIAL
    ↓
CLAIM / CLEAR / DISBURSE
    ↓
COSTBAND COMMITMENT
    ↓
PROGRESS
```

with **derivation budget as one intrinsic constrained lane and fission as one structural consequence**, and **physical movement as the clearest spatial witness rather than the semantic definition**.

### 0.2 Recommended disposition

**Design direction: favorable, but canonize the composition law rather than a new object.**

The case is strong enough to justify a dedicated semantic falsifier before 7.1/8.2 freeze narrower interpretations. A successful proof should amend the relevant design language so that movement, derivation, resource satisfaction, and contention all consume the same intrinsic constrained-action protocol. The vector/multi-resource extension and tiled executor should remain separately falsifiable research hypotheses.

## 1. What ActionBand means — and what it does not mean

### 1.1 Working definition

> **ActionBand is the intrinsic StemThing pattern by which competing field-derived potentials are valued, translated into resource-backed claims, cleared against constrained capacity, quantized by CostBand into determinate progress, and applied as ordinary state change or an existing boundary-authorized structural transition.**

The term is provisional. The important object of review is the law, not the name.

### 1.2 It is not a fifth leg

The four StemThing legs remain:

```text
participate
act
originate
receive
```

ActionBand uses those legs. It does not add anatomy.

- **participate:** pressure, claims, deficits, grants, and balances are ordinary lanes;
- **act:** CostBand turns granted value into executable depth;
- **originate:** overlays/directives deform potential or author transition effects;
- **receive:** grants and directives arrive through existing RF/event seams.

### 1.3 It is not a planner

ActionBand does not contain:

- a destination registry;
- an `ActionKind`;
- a CPU goal selector;
- a behavior tree;
- an action graph with independent lifecycle;
- a combat/economy/diplomacy service;
- a movement-specific pathfinder.

The destination/goal must be **derived from the field and admitted topology**, not supplied as semantic authority.

### 1.4 It is not synonymous with CostBand

CostBand is a crucial hinge inside the cycle, but it does not subsume clearing.

- **clearing:** how simultaneous claims share bounded capacity;
- **CostBand:** how granted value funds determinate work/action quanta;
- **contention:** the condition where simultaneous claims cannot all clear;
- **unresolved claim:** what a claimant wanted but did not receive;
- **remainder:** granted/held value not yet sufficient for another CostBand quantum.

Those distinctions survive the unification.

## 2. Action potential precedes action identity

This is the most important new design candidate in this revision.

> **A StemThing should not require an authored destination in order to act. Its current state, deficits, opportunities, STEAD fields, PALMA impedance, relation fields, and authored/user overlays define one or more potential surfaces over admitted adjacency. Candidate action directions are gradients on those surfaces.**

Physical movement made this easiest to see, but the law is broader.

### 2.1 Physical movement is the canonical witness

A fleet may observe:

```text
destination attraction
terrain impedance
fuel cost
enemy threat
supply support
orders / player overlays
```

These deform the local potential surface. The next movement direction is a gradient over that surface.

A user waypoint should therefore be interpreted as:

> **an overlay that deforms the field so the waypoint becomes an attractor**

rather than a privileged engine-side `Destination` instruction.

### 2.2 Goals are attractors, not semantic objects

Outside physical movement, "destination" is usually not a coordinate at all.

A population with a food deficit may have competing attractors:

- nearby scarce food;
- distant abundant food;
- expensive imports;
- politically preferred suppliers;
- substitution;
- rationing.

A polity may have simultaneous diplomatic gradients:

- trade opportunity toward one actor;
- threat away from another;
- alliance opportunity toward a third;
- internal isolation pressure;
- fiscal pressure against intervention.

A production system may face:

- food scarcity;
- housing scarcity;
- weapons demand;
- consumer demand;
- capital scarcity;
- labor scarcity.

ActionBand must permit the **goal itself to emerge from the gradient competition**.

### 2.3 Existing topology is sufficient in principle

No generic `ActionGraph` should be invented.

Candidate progress may occur over already-admitted topology:

- `GridOffsets` for spatial locality;
- `LinkGraph` for supply, diplomatic, trade, communication, or other authored relations;
- the recursive SimThing tree for hierarchical resource satisfaction;
- an admitted tier ladder for derivation;
- ordinary consequence bindings where no separate graph is required.

The field-sweep substrate already demonstrated one generic authored map/fold/post path over both grid and link adjacency. ActionBand should consume that generality rather than minting a new topology authority.

## 3. Competing goals: EML arbitrates gradients

A realistic StemThing often faces multiple contradictory potential gradients at once.

For candidate progress edge/transition `e` and field channel `k`, define conceptually:

```text
delta_phi[e,k]
```

as the local potential change along that candidate.

The engine need not know what any `k` means.

An authored EML law can evaluate:

```text
score_e = F(
    delta_phi[e,*],
    deficits,
    prices,
    risk,
    relation/prior state,
    overlays,
    existing commitment,
    local capacity,
    ...
)
```

### 3.1 No mandated goal-selection regime

Different authored programs may yield:

- hard best-candidate selection;
- proportional distribution of effort;
- priority-ordered behavior;
- stabilized softmax-like effort sharing;
- risk-sensitive or reserve-sensitive behavior.

EXP makes smooth winner-take-most / diversified weighting practical:

```text
weight_i = EXP(beta * (score_i - max_score))
```

Small `beta` can spread effort; large `beta` approaches winner-take-most. This is an authored numerical regime, not an engine enum.

### 3.2 Entropy as a generic action observable

LN/EXP make a generic action-distribution entropy available:

```text
H = -SUM(p_i * LN(p_i))
```

Low entropy means one attractor dominates. High entropy means several directions are similarly attractive.

The core does not interpret that as indecision, diversification, strategic ambiguity, migration uncertainty, or supply flexibility. Those are vendor/domain meanings. The generic quantity is useful for diagnostics, corpus, or further EML feedback.

### 3.3 Commitment persistence prevents thrash

Without persistence, near-equal goals can flip every generation.

Existing commitment should therefore be allowed as ordinary EML input:

```text
new_score =
    candidate_score
  + persistence_weight * existing_commitment
  - switching_cost
```

Finite commitment memory may decay through EXP:

```text
memory_next =
    memory_now * EXP(-lambda * dt)
  + current_commitment
```

This produces hysteresis without a bespoke `CurrentAction` state machine.

## 4. Intrinsic constrained clearing — contention is the oversubscribed posture

The earlier version proposed an intrinsic contention CostBand stub. The Fable/Sol critique correctly identified that as over-canonization.

The stronger law is simpler:

> **Every constrained resource-bearing parent intrinsically supports claim → clear → disburse. Contention is what that same facility looks like when claims exceed the feasible supply.**

### 4.1 Trivial and contested clearing are one path

```text
claims fit capacity
        ↓
ordinary clear

claims exceed capacity
        ↓
same clear
        ↓
unresolved claims
        ↓
contention observation
```

No `if contention { create_arena }` semantic step is required.

### 4.2 Generic unresolved quantity

For scalar bounded supply:

```text
U_i = max(claim_i - allocation_i, 0)
```

For richer feasible regions, `U_i` is the admitted unresolved portion of the clearing result.

`U_i > 0` means only that the claim did not clear.

It does **not** mean:

- hostility;
- combat;
- inequality;
- adversariality;
- attrition.

Those are possible higher-order interpretations or consequences.

### 4.3 `U` and CostBand `R` must remain distinct

This is a binding correction from the aggregate review.

```text
U = requested value that was NOT granted
R = granted/held value left after N affordable CostBand quanta
```

Conflating them destroys both contention and CostBand semantics.

### 4.4 Clearing economics remain authored data

The substrate provides constrained resolution, not an ideology.

Admitted numerical clearing may include, where justified:

- proportional;
- authored priority;
- price-clearing;
- deterministic ordered service;
- later numerical laws proven through the same generic surface.

No physical-row or vector iteration order may become policy.

## 5. Attrition and adversariality are temporal persistence, not clearing identity

The current 8.2 row lists attrition beside proportional/priority/price-clearing. This revision recommends that DA re-open that factorization.

A cleaner candidate is:

```text
current claims
    ↓
current clearing law
    ↓
unresolved claims U
    ↓
ordinary observation / STEAD
    ↓
EML persistence valuation
    ↓
CostBand-funded continued commitment
    ↓
resource/state consequences
    ↓
next generation
    ↓
new claims
```

Under this model:

- **contention** is current infeasibility;
- **adversariality** is the degree of costly refusal/accommodation encoded in continued commitment;
- **attrition** is resource loss/expenditure caused by persistent incompatible commitment across generations.

No `AttritionResolutionRule` is needed unless a real consumer proves a distinct same-generation clearing law is necessary.

### 5.1 Generation pacing is non-negotiable

Never introduce:

```text
clear
→ persist
→ mutate
→ re-clear
→ persist
→ convergence
```

within one generation.

The recursive causal fabric advances because generations recur. That preserves bounded execution and the already-settled automaton pacing.

## 6. CostBand as a universal work quantizer

CostBand's settled scalar algebra is:

```text
N = floor(V / C)
R = V - N*C
V = N*C + R
```

The earlier view emphasized queues and discrete production. The deeper interpretation is:

> **CostBand converts granted/available value into an exact number of determinate work or action quanta while carrying unused value forward without loss.**

### 6.1 Existing and candidate interpretations

| Apparent domain behavior | CostBand interpretation |
|---|---|
| boolean action | depth limited to `N ∈ {0,1}` |
| production | affordable output/work units |
| movement | affordable progress quanta |
| repair | repair-work quanta |
| construction | build-work quanta |
| derivation | affordable descendant units |
| migration | relocation/progress quanta |
| command | executable command depth |
| policy intervention | funded intervention quanta |
| service throughput | jobs/requests served |
| reservation | exact committed capacity tokens |

The core sees only priced work.

### 6.2 Pseudo-continuous action deserves a probe

A continuous EML rate can accumulate into `V` until a CostBand quantum clears:

```text
rate -> accumulator V -> CostBand
```

Example:

```text
C = 1
rate = 0.13 / generation

V=.13 -> N=0 R=.13
...
V=1.04 -> N=1 R=.04
```

This behaves like an exact carried pulse-density representation of continuous desired work.

It should **not** be canonized as the representation for every continuous process. STEAD diffusion and arbitrary continuous state evolution remain continuous. But it is worth testing how broad the boundary between continuous pressure and determinate work can become, especially because the CostBand kernel shape is highly parallel and regular.

## 9. Multi-resource atomicity — Vector CostBand as a research extension

Multi-resource atomicity is a real challenge and one reason movement should remain a serious workplan witness. The correct first response is not a transaction subsystem.

A natural extension of CostBand is:

```text
N = min_j floor(V_j / C_j)
R_j = V_j - N*C_j
```

where `j` ranges over required resource lanes.

The scalar CostBand is the width-1 case.

### 9.1 Example

One progress quantum costs:

```text
movement_points = 1
fuel            = 2
supply          = 0.5
```

Provisional grants:

```text
movement_points = 7
fuel            = 11
supply          = 2
```

Then:

```text
floor(7/1)   = 7
floor(11/2)  = 5
floor(2/.5)  = 4

N = 4
```

Exactly four action quanta can commit across all lanes.

### 9.2 Alignment with tier price vectors

The StemThing residency/derivation design already defines tiers as price vectors. That makes this extension unusually coherent for derivation:

```text
derivation capacity
+ residency capacity
+ other admitted hard resources
    ↓
one common executable descendant depth N
```

### 9.3 Provisional grants and the holding account

Independent resource clears can produce provisional grants held in-flight:

```text
resource clears
    ↓
provisional grants
    ↓
in-flight holding account
    ↓
Vector CostBand computes common N
    ↓
consume N*C_j on every required lane
    ↓
unused provisional values return
```

Per-resource conservation remains sovereign.

### 9.4 Do not make ActionBand depend on this probe

Vector CostBand is **not** a prerequisite for ActionBand semantic admission.

First prove the generic action cycle with scalar claims. Then test vector atomicity independently.

### 9.5 Do not same-generation re-clear returned excess

Returned provisional capacity should become available at the next normal resolution opportunity. Immediate iterative redistribution risks creating a convergence solver and violating generation pacing.

## 10. Structural mutation remains boundary physics

ActionBand may authorize structural consequences; it must not perform structural surgery inside EML or the tile executor.

Examples that remain barrier-owned include:

- fission/fusion;
- slot/extent minting;
- reparenting/membership changes;
- compaction/remap;
- physical residency growth;
- schema/registry changes.

The numerical side may only produce the admitted request/grant needed to authorize them.

### 10.1 Why this boundary remains valuable

Structural mutation has costs and invariants the numerical executor should not own:

- placement may fail;
- extents must remain disjoint;
- logical identity/remap history is authoritative;
- allocation may require device/host interaction;
- physical memory growth is not guaranteed;
- replay must preserve structural ordering.

### 10.2 The goal is not "never allocate"

The stronger operational goal is:

> **No hot structural ActionBand should discover its memory requirement at the instant it commits.**

Controlled growth/remap at admission or explicit epochs remains lawful.

## 11. Predictive residency and the session VRAM envelope

The ActionBand/derivation unification makes memory demand itself more predictable.

After scenario parsing and session admission, SimThing should be able to derive a useful engine-owned residency envelope.

### 11.1 Three useful footprint values

```text
INITIAL LIVE
    bytes/resources required by the admitted starting state

LAWFUL STEADY CAP
    maximum resident state permitted by admitted root capacities,
    tier vocabulary, and residency economics

STRUCTURAL PEAK
    steady cap plus temporary duplication/scratch/in-flight space
    required for remap, compaction, double buffering, uploads, etc.
```

"Minimum/maximum VRAM" is too coarse because the temporary structural peak may exceed the steady resident maximum.

### 11.2 Initial footprint can be strongly determined

Admission already knows or can know:

- current SimThing count;
- admitted lane sets;
- frozen tier vocabulary;
- current field registrations;
- adjacency structures;
- EML tables/programs;
- initial memberships;
- required double buffers;
- residency classes;
- known fixed scratch classes.

The initial engine-owned byte request should therefore be close to exact.

### 11.3 Recursive derivation need not make the maximum explode

If residency is a conserved root capacity:

```text
root capacity
    ↓ partitions
children
    ↓ partitions
grandchildren
```

the same capacity cannot be granted twice.

The physical ceiling is bounded by the root grant rather than by multiplying per-level maxima.

This is one of the strongest arguments for malloc-as-RF.

### 11.4 A finite maximum requires a finite root/device grant

If the root residency budget may grow without bound, no finite scenario-derived maximum exists.

A finite envelope therefore requires one of:

- a finite authored/admitted root capacity;
- a finite device-relative session grant;
- or a current envelope plus explicit epoch-growth policy.

The design should not pretend an unbounded session has a calculable final maximum.

### 11.5 Tier vocabulary makes the cap tractable

With a small frozen set of tier price vectors, admission can solve or conservatively bound:

```text
maximize resident bytes
subject to admitted conserved capacities
```

without enumerating every future descendant instance.

### 11.6 Do not reserve the entire maximum by default

A session with:

```text
initial live = 3 GiB
lawful cap   = 14 GiB
```

need not allocate 14 GiB immediately.

Instead:

```text
live
+ forecast headroom
+ structural safety margin
```

can grow at controlled epochs.

### 11.7 Action gradients can drive pre-grant

Growth velocity is one predictor. ActionBand exposes a richer one:

```text
potential steepens
    ↓
action score rises
    ↓
expected claim rises
    ↓
residency/structural capacity pre-granted
    ↓
free -> in_flight
    ↓
later action commits without surprise allocation
```

If preallocation misses, the lawful failure should be `N = 0` / action waits, not an unsafe hot-path realloc.

## 12. Hierarchical-spatial causal fabric — now as a consequence of ActionBand

The desired fabric does not require a new recursive structure.

### 12.1 Vertical recursion: RF

Claims and deficits reduce upward. Each constrained parent applies the same generic clear/disburse semantics. Grants return downward.

### 12.2 Lateral recursion: STEAD / PALMA / field sweeps

Field pressure propagates over admitted spatial or link topology.

### 12.3 Commitment: CostBand

Granted value becomes determinate paid progress.

### 12.4 Opposed committed flow: Gu-Yang

Gu-Yang remains the instantaneous conservative stall/saturation mechanism.

### 12.5 Time: generations

Persistence, hysteresis, escalation, accommodation, and attrition emerge across generations.

```text
state
  ↓
field potential
  ↓
ActionBand claims
  ↓
clear/disburse
  ↓
CostBand progress
  ↓
state / flow consequences
  ↓
STEAD / Gu-Yang
  ↓
next generation
```

The complex hierarchical-spatial causal fabric is therefore fractal reuse of one StemThing law, not a separate fabric object.

## 15. EML tiling — revised performance thesis after Fable/Sol

The semantic ActionBand proposal must stand without a tile backend.

The performance question is separate:

> **Can the one EML language and the canonical ActionBand/RF workload be physically lowered into locality-aware tiles that match or outperform traditional/native GPU implementations at SimThing scale?**

5.14 materially improves the feasibility of this question because arithmetic meaning is now language-owned; a future tile backend should be only another faithful lowering, never another evidence/semantic axis.

### 15.1 Do not begin by building a tensor VM

The first measured debt remains the 5.7 Gu-Yang locality failure:

```text
generated JIT:
    repeated global neighbor gathers

bespoke:
    workgroup-local neighborhood tile
    reuse loaded cells
```

The first probe should therefore be the smallest structural correction:

```text
same EML
same fold order
same outputs
same SSA/JIT semantics
    ↓
shared/workgroup-local ingress
```

If this closes most of the gap, do not overbuild the architecture.

### 15.2 General tile hypothesis

Only residual measured debt should justify the broader tile executor.

A software tile is a physical container such as:

```text
EmlTileClass {
    instance_width,
    operation_width,
    stage_depth,
    ingress_slots,
    scratch_slots,
    egress_slots,
}
```

It may exploit:

1. many SimThings running the same program;
2. independent EML DAG nodes at one dependency level;
3. limited internal parallelism inside expensive admitted primitives where semantics permit.

The first axis is expected to dominate.

### 15.3 ActionBand provides the canonical workload

Instead of an artificial inequality-specific shader, the useful tile workload is:

```text
potential / gradient columns
        ↓
EML action scoring
        ↓
claim pricing
        ↓
RF reduce-up
        ↓
constrained clear
        ↓
disburse-down
        ↓
CostBand
        ↓
post-grant EML / effect columns
```

This repeats at massive scale and across many domains.

### 15.4 Multi-channel field reuse remains a high-value target

Once a neighborhood is loaded on-chip, evaluate multiple lawful field channels before eviction where semantics allow:

```text
one topology gather
    ├─ potential A
    ├─ potential B
    ├─ potential C
    └─ Gu-Yang/comparative work
```

Each channel retains its own authored law and canonical fold order.

### 17.2 Gradient-authority falsifier

Every witness should have multiple competing attractors.

Change only an overlay: progress changes again.

Create two strong opposing goals.

- one winner;
- the other winner;
- soft effort split;
- persistence against one-tick noise;
- redirection when a required resource is exhausted.

### 17.4 Generation-pacing falsifier

Prove the implementation cannot recursively clear/persist/re-clear to convergence inside a generation.

### 17.5 Category-boundary falsifier

A derived signal must not become conserved merely because it feeds an action. A claim must not be mistaken for an allocation. `U` and `R` must remain independently observable.

### 17.6 Structural-boundary falsifier

A resource-backed structural action may authorize only an existing boundary request. EML/tile code cannot directly mutate tree identity/layout.

## 22. Canonization candidates for DA review

These are drafting candidates only.

### 22.1 Action-potential law

> **Action potential precedes action identity.** A StemThing does not require an authored destination to act. Its local state, deficits, opportunities, propagated STEAD/field values, and authored/user overlays define potential surfaces over admitted topology. Candidate progress directions are gradients on those surfaces. Domain labels compile away; the core receives only numerical fields, adjacency, resource lanes, and admitted consequence bindings.

### 22.2 ActionBand composition law

> **A resource-backed StemThing action is field-derived potential converted into constrained progress.** EML values competing candidate gradients and produces claims. Constrained parents clear and disburse ordinary resource lanes. CostBand converts granted value into determinate progress depth. Numeric consequences return to ordinary lanes; structural consequences execute only through the existing boundary. The next generation observes the resulting state and may continue, redirect, or dissolve the commitment.

### 22.3 Intrinsic constrained-clearing law

> **Constrained disbursement is intrinsic to every resource-bearing StemThing parent.** Claims that fit capacity and claims that oversubscribe capacity use the same resolution surface. Contention is the ordinary observed condition where simultaneous claims cannot all clear; it is not a separately-authored mode or participant taxonomy.

### 22.4 CostBand hinge law

> **CostBand is the singular quantizer from granted constrained value to determinate resource-consuming work.** Observation and derived potential are free. A paid action exists only where admitted constrained value is committed through CostBand. Boolean depth one is the degenerate case; wider action depths are the same mechanism.

### 22.5 Temporal-adversariality law

> **Adversariality is not required to create contention.** Persistent unresolved claims may influence later EML valuation and receive continued CostBand funding. Attrition and costly refusal emerge across generations from maintained incompatible commitments; Gu-Yang remains an instantaneous conservative counterflow/stall mechanism rather than a conflict-memory service.

### 22.6 Derivation law

> **Derivation is ActionBand progress whose product is another SimThing.** Derivation and residency capacity are ordinary constrained price-vector lanes. A successful CostBand draw authorizes the existing boundary to fission/place the admitted descendant. No derivation production subsystem exists beside the tree.

### 22.7 Structural-boundary law

> **ActionBand authorizes structure; it does not mutate structure numerically.** Fission, reparenting, residency growth, compaction, and remap remain boundary/epoch physics with their existing identity, placement, and history obligations. Predictive claims should move capacity in advance so hot transitions consume prepared grants rather than discover memory demand.

### 22.8 Tile law

> **EML tiling is execution-only.** A tile is a physical packing of already-admitted EML/lane work over stable SimThing rows. It may change where values are loaded, cached, fused, and scheduled; it may not change semantic arithmetic, canonical fold order, identity, history, resource law, goal selection law, or generation ordering.

## 23. Does this justify full intrinsic unification?

### 23.1 Case in favor

The architectural case is now stronger than in the first draft because three facilities independently converged on the same form:

#### CostBand

Already universalizes the sink/action boundary:

```text
observation
→ resource-backed action
```

#### Derivation/residency

Already treats child creation and memory capacity as priced constrained draws:

```text
growth pressure
→ deficit
→ CostBand
→ grant
→ fission
```

#### Movement / action

Was originally conceived as a generic commitment-to-progress mechanism and already binds physical movement costs to CostBand:

```text
field pressure
→ gradient
→ committed progress
```

8.2 supplies the missing generic claim/clear/disburse middle.

Putting these together removes rather than adds concepts.

### 23.2 What "full intrinsic unification" should mean

It should **not** mean:

```text
struct ActionBand { ... }
```

must exist everywhere.

It should mean the core design guarantees one lawful path for resource-backed determinate action:

```text
potential
→ valuation
→ claim
→ clear
→ grant
→ CostBand
→ progress/consequence
```

and specialized systems may not bypass it with parallel sink, planner, contention, movement-cost, or derivation-budget mechanisms.

### 23.3 What remains outside

The unification does not absorb:

- topology/adjacency admission;
- structural placement physics;
- EML semantic definition;
- field physics;
- conservation oracle;
- external device allocation reality;
- authoring of prices/weights/causal laws;
- non-consuming observations.

Those boundaries are why the unification remains tractable.

### 23.4 Judgment

**Yes: this now appears to justify intrinsic unification as a core StemThing composition law, subject to falsification before canonization.**

The strongest reason is not elegance. It is that the existing workplan already contains four independently-designed mechanisms that are forced to meet at the same seams:

```text
STEAD/PALMA potential
CostBand
8.2 claim/clear/disburse
derivation/residency budget
```

Leaving them conceptually disparate creates more future branching pressure than unifying them.

The primary risk is overreach: forcing every continuous process through CostBand, inventing a second action graph, or turning a useful composition law into a heavy runtime object. The falsifiers above are designed to reject exactly those failures.
