## 1. North star

**0.0.8.8 rehearses the recursive StemThing RF Triad under a real integrated application.**
The subject under test and refinement is the substrate landed in 0.0.8.7: the exact
constrained market (Π^exact_C ∘ R — Q149 live basis, u32 caps, saturate-and-redistribute
closure, rotated ties, neutral degeneration), the two recursion axes (spatial same-generation
forwarding; temporal once-mint `authored + f(U)`), the complete stream-membership lifecycle
(zero-member canon, neutral termination facts, survivor-subset partial mint, fault poisoning),
the posture singularity (one resident-qualified market, one verdict, CPU oracle), the E8
adapter-seal qualification, and the resident execution spine.

ClauseThing, the scenario hydrator/projection, and the Studio client are **conforming
instruments**: they exist to author, install, execute, observe, and measure that substrate
faithfully. Their modernization is judged by paradigm fidelity and by what they let the
substrate demonstrate — never by their own feature ambitions.

The rehearsal delivers four things together:

1. **A demonstrated substrate**: `scenarios/stellaristhing_base.clause` — two factions turning
   finite resource flow into funded fleet birth, paid movement, finite hostile damage, and
   durable continuation, all through the ordinary resident session in the current Studio, with
   no hidden substitute model. This is the representative workload instrument.
2. **A converged ingress**: every live pre-StemThing route (ClauseThing lowering, projection,
   hydration, the Studio bridge) converged to canonical StemThing semantics or retired.
3. **Complete exposure**: every 0.0.8.7-landed system exposed to the test environment; every
   load-bearing system characterized on the current path with a valid instrument.
4. **A performance dossier**: reproducible current-path measurements locating the natural
   constraint surface of an irregular authored hierarchy, with kernel optimization candidates
   left visibly unopened until the Owner's release.

The finish is specific: open the delivered ClauseScript file in the current Studio, watch two
factions build, move, and fight fleets through resource-funded behavior, change the authored
configuration without engine edits, restore and continue, and reproduce the natural-hierarchy
performance findings for that same implementation.

## 2. Pillars (binding at commitment)

- **P1 — Subject singularity (Owner Clause A).** The recursive StemThing RF Triad is the
  subject; every other surface conforms. Application features cannot trade against substrate
  truth; feature ambitions never justify changing StemThing law to fit a legacy path. A domain
  noun is content vocabulary; it never selects a new engine algorithm.
- **P2 — Converge or retire (Owner Clause B).** Every live surface predating StemThing/RF-Triad
  is workshopped to the recursive paradigm: it converges to canonical semantics or retires
  through the ordinary lifecycle. None survives as an alternate semantic authority. The
  constitutional surface census is the single binding worklist; preserve-as-compat retains a
  supported surface through canonical semantics and can never shelter an alternate execution
  authority; a blocked ingress stays blocked until lawfully resolved. Historical stage-only and
  negative fixtures retain their historical contracts as records.
- **P3 — Total exposure (Owner Clause C).** Every system landed in 0.0.8.7 must be exposed to
  the test environment (natural-application workload and/or synthetic isolation). Load-bearing
  systems additionally require a valid instrument, a current-path baseline, and candidate
  eligibility in the deferred optimization branch. Closeout permits zero unexposed landed
  systems and zero uncharacterized load-bearing systems. Invisibility never completes anything.
- **P4 — Authored growth (Owner Clause D).** The scenario and Studio are living instruments:
  within a rung they may grow new authored systems, resource flows, UI elements, and testing
  regimes without per-feature DA relays, graduating through the admitted class families. Growth
  flows only through admitted doors; growth that exercises no substrate carries no exposure
  credit; rung exit-proofs are floors, not ceilings.
- **P5 — Flattened graduation.** Majority-Orchestrator graduation for ordinary implementation,
  content, UI, and measurement work is an explicit design objective. DA interventions
  concentrate at CHARTER (where the whole worklist is ruled once) and at true authority
  changes. Protected census/anchor/gate surfaces stay DA-reserved; CHARTER pre-authorizes the
  converge-or-retire worklist so executing a recorded transition is mechanical.
- **P6 — Measurement truth.** Performance comparisons bind to named workload revisions; no
  comparison spans an unpinned workload change; no performance claim is obtained by changing
  the workload. The frozen Phase-14.1 comparator stays byte-frozen; the Phase-15 composite
  `T_R` is separately versioned. Every research row carries the six status fields (§5.7).
  Historical numbers remain historical; a stale instrument is unresolved evidence, not a pass.
- **P7 — Sequencing.** Characterize the natural application before optimizing. The kernel
  optimization branch opens only on the Owner's release naming the measured constraint on the
  unchanged authored workload. Deferring optimization defers neither exposure nor
  characterization.

## 4. Binding laws (carry from day one)

1. **Precedence.** The latest unified SimThing laws supersede all older laws. The Core Design
   is the highest design source; the 0.0.8.7 Unification ADR is the landed API contract; this
   workplan binds below them; records are non-binding. Old closure wording, an unsupported
   legacy door, or a passing historical fixture never justifies weakening the current paradigm.
   Unamended conflict is a STOP.
2. **Subject law.** Substrate truth outranks application convenience (P1). No economy,
   movement, combat, or logistics manager acquires a fifth executive leg; no UI readback
   supplies economic authority; no second IR, registry, planner, or field solver.
3. **Admitted doors and complete lifecycles.** Every capability flows through admitted
   StemThing authority. A genuine generic gap gets its own D-scope rung landing WITH its first
   consumer in the same window, lifecycle complete (Owner no-deferred-capabilities law). The
   policy stage applies once; no consumer re-runs RF/Triad to improve an outcome; resource
   remainder, impairment, and delivery shortfall never become U by renaming.
4. **Measurement pinning.** Workload revisions are named (source/profile digests); no
   comparison spans an unpinned workload change; fixed-generation comparisons establish
   semantic equivalence and per-generation cost, wall-clock runs establish responsiveness. The
   inherited tiled-gather limits (≤1.25× median, ≤1.5× worst vs the comparable retained
   reference) stand unless explicitly re-adjudicated. Instrument repair, missing measurement,
   and unmet ratio have distinct dispositions.
5. **Exposure law.** No 0.0.8.7-landed system may end the track unexposed; no load-bearing
   system uncharacterized (P3). A row counts exposed only through an actual executed path and
   witness — never a name in a diagram or a compile-only path.
6. **Execution law.** Resident execution is ordinary; CPU waits are opt-in; the CPU form is
   oracle only; one admitted verdict across postures; touched-generation faults are fail-stop
   with no invented rollback; observation is bounded asynchronous egress with typed exhaustion.
   Any touched adapter-sealed bundle follows the E8 pin-roll with clean-checkout
   reproducibility proof under the required profiling tuple.
7. **Gate law.** NEVER merge on a red check. Gate repairs are separate DA PRs landed between
   rungs; no agent edits gate code to pass its own work. The certificate of record is the
   DA-side full-workspace cargo test battery; hosted CI runs no cargo tests, and hosted green
   never substitutes for owner-local GPU/Studio evidence on rows that require it.
8. **Growth law.** P4 growth graduates through class envelopes with exposure credit only for
   substrate actually exercised; measurement campaigns pin their workload revision regardless
   of growth cadence; new test regimes may renew parked 0.0.8.7 referees on fresh wall-clock
   leases — never immortality, no zombie orphans.
9. **Frozen-referee law.** A referee asserting superseded law is corrected to the ruled law at
   equal or greater strength — never weakened, deleted, or satisfied by rollback.
10. **Anchor law.** Anchor payloads are extracted without banners: every anchored section is
    self-contained law. Canonization/repoint is atomic with the doc edit; range anchors are
    re-verified by DIRECT extraction after any edit to their doc.

## 5.3 Construction contract (F2 ruling)

**Model 1 first:** delivered inputs become explicitly owned work-in-progress stock in ordinary
SimThings/lanes, recoverable until the recipe consumes them; storage, cancellation, capacity,
and placement dispositions all admitted; the consuming vertical modernizes to canonical
ownership semantics. **Discriminator:** two projects competing for one unit of each of two
inputs, with separate allocation outcomes favoring opposite projects — the model must explain
ownership, progress, cancellation, and why the declared product profile can eventually
complete. **Model 2 (atomic multi-lane recipe commitment) opens ONLY if Model 1 fails that
falsifier**, as an explicit generic capability amendment landing with its first consumer and
complete contention/refusal/cancellation/release/restart semantics in the same window. Neither
model converts incomplete inputs into irreversible build credit; neither creates a
construction-specific allocator. **Placement is part of the contract:** when the residency
commitment is acquired, when materials are consumed, and the disposition when placement
becomes unavailable; a touched generation fault stays fail-stop. Repeated completions mint
fresh instances through the existing structural door (`AddChild`/`Remove`/`Reparent`) and bind
properties, overlays, membership, observations, and child structure — a frozen template plus a
completion counter proves nothing.

## 5.4 Movement contract (F3 ruling)

The destination is the ActionBand target; the born PALMA cost-to-go at the subject's locus
supplies remaining impedance; funded movement work pays it; progress is proportional to local
paid work. **Partial-work identity:** paid partial work binds to (subject identity, target
revision, topology revision, authorized adjacent transition). **Price law:** the local price is
FROZEN for the currently authorized transition only; untraversed transitions reprice
prospectively. Retarget, cancellation, edge removal, blockage, source death, and placement
refusal each have an authored disposition; paid progress never transfers silently and no work
unit is spent twice or lost unaccounted. Consume signed native flux in the authorized
direction; exactly one declared conserved-progress bound; transit capacity, stored movement
work, CostBand remainder, partial local progress, and RF unresolved demand remain five
distinct quantities. Equal lawful descents take an admitted deterministic selection or an
explicit refusal — physical storage order never decides. A finite PALMA sweep is not a global
shortest-path guarantee: out-of-horizon/unreachable targets produce admitted refusal/stall,
never fabricated distance. One-local-step structural ingress is preserved; no route sequence,
predecessor table, or movement-owned planner. **Reference witness:** transitions costing 3
then 4 work units under production 2/generation with bounded remainder — first crossing at
generation 2 (1 left), second at generation 4; a mid-payment cost/target change accounts for
every unit.
